STORM QUEST - ANSWER KEY (for teachers)
=======================================
In the game, question and option order is shuffled. Match by wording. Correct answer marked [X].

LEVEL 1 - SKY OF SECRETS
------------------------

Q1. What is pressure?
  [X] A. Force on each unit of area
  [ ] B. Area added to force
  [ ] C. The speed of air
  [ ] D. The weight of water only
  Why: Pressure is force per unit area.

Q2. During the day, a sea breeze usually blows…
  [X] A. From sea to land
  [ ] B. From land to sea
  [ ] C. Only upwards
  [ ] D. From north to south
  Why: Land heats faster, warm air rises over land, and cooler air moves in from the sea.

Q3. At night, a land breeze usually blows…
  [ ] A. From sea to land
  [X] B. From land to sea
  [ ] C. Only towards the sky
  [ ] D. There is no breeze
  Why: Land cools faster, so air moves from land towards the sea.

Q4. What does air near the surface do in a HIGH-pressure area?
  [X] A. Spreads outwards
  [ ] B. Flows inward and rises
  [ ] C. Stops moving
  [ ] D. Turns into water
  Why: Near the surface, air spreads out from a high-pressure region towards lower pressure.

Q5. What happens to air that flows into a LOW-pressure area?
  [ ] A. It sinks into the ground
  [X] B. It flows inward and rises
  [ ] C. It freezes
  [ ] D. It disappears
  Why: Air flows inward towards a low-pressure area and rises, which can form clouds and rain.

Q6. Why can very fast wind lift a weak roof?
  [X] A. It creates lower pressure above the roof than inside
  [ ] B. It makes the roof heavier
  [ ] C. It cools the building
  [ ] D. It removes all air inside
  Why: Fast wind lowers pressure above the roof; higher pressure inside can push the roof upward.

LEVEL 2 - EYE OF THE STORM
--------------------------

Q1. What causes thunder?
  [X] A. Rapid heating and expansion of air
  [ ] B. Rain cooling the sea
  [ ] C. Clouds turning dark
  [ ] D. The wind stopping
  Why: Lightning heats nearby air rapidly. The air expands and produces thunder.

Q2. Where do tropical cyclones form?
  [X] A. Over warm ocean water
  [ ] B. Over deserts only
  [ ] C. On snowy mountains
  [ ] D. Inside buildings
  Why: Tropical cyclones form over warm ocean water.

Q3. What should we do when officials advise cyclone evacuation?
  [ ] A. Go to the beach
  [X] B. Move to a designated shelter
  [ ] C. Stand under a tree
  [ ] D. Stay near windows
  Why: Follow official alerts and move to a designated shelter when advised.

Q4. How does a thunderstorm cloud form?
  [ ] A. Cold dry air sinks
  [X] B. Warm, moist air rises, cools and condenses
  [ ] C. Wind stops blowing
  [ ] D. Snow melts on mountains
  Why: Rising warm, moist air cools and condenses into tall storm clouds.

Q5. What kind of pressure is found at the centre of a cyclone?
  [X] A. Very low pressure
  [ ] B. Very high pressure
  [ ] C. No air at all
  [ ] D. Pressure that never changes
  Why: A cyclone forms around a very low-pressure centre.

Q6. Where are the strongest winds and heaviest rain of a cyclone found?
  [ ] A. In the calm eye
  [X] B. In the eyewall around the eye
  [ ] C. Far away from the storm
  [ ] D. Only over land
  Why: The eyewall around the eye has the strongest winds and heaviest rain.
